# There is no ClearHire browser extension

**ClearHire does not ship a browser extension, and there is nothing to install.** If you have arrived
here looking for one, that is the whole answer — nothing on this page describes something you can use
today.

This page exists because it used to say the opposite, and a page that quietly disappears leaves
anyone who bookmarked it or found it in a search result with no explanation.

## What there used to be

An earlier version of ClearHire had a desktop Chrome add-on in development. It captured a LinkedIn
profile, auto-filled job-application forms on three sites, and kept a local list of jobs you had
looked at. **It was never published to any store.** Nobody outside development ever installed it, and
no data ever passed through it.

## Why it is not coming back as part of this

ClearHire was rebuilt in 2026, and the rebuild deliberately left the extension out of scope. The
reasoning was narrow and worth stating plainly: an extension is a separate product with its own store
review, its own permission model, its own update cycle and its own privacy surface. Bundling one into
a web app's scope makes both later and neither better.

That is a decision about ClearHire's scope, not a judgement that the idea is bad. If an extension is
ever built, it will be announced as its own thing rather than reappearing quietly on this page.

## What to use instead

Everything the prototype did has a home in the app itself:

- **Keeping track of where you applied** — [application tracking](/features/applications-tracking)
  records the role, the company, the stage and what you sent, whether or not you applied through
  ClearHire.
- **Not retyping your details** — your [profile](/features/profile-management) is written once and
  every resume is generated from it, so the retyping the extension was meant to save is already gone
  at the source.
- **Checking a resume against a posting** — the
  [ATS resume checker](/guides/ats-resume-checker) does that in the browser, with no install.

## One page that does still exist

ClearHire keeps a published privacy page for the extension at
[clearhire.aoneahsan.com/privacy-extension](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/privacy-extension). It is
kept because it was once a public URL and removing a privacy page is not something to do quietly —
not because there is an extension behind it.
