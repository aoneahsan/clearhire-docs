# Requesting a Paid Plan

ClearHire has three plans: **Free, Pro and Team**. Pro and Team are available by arrangement: you **request** the plan
in the app, and the ClearHire team sets it up on your account. There is no card form. Free stays a full plan that does
not run out, and employment verification is on it.

## Where to request a plan

Sign in and open **Settings → Plan** (`/settings/plan`). The Pro and Team cards each carry **Request this plan**, which
asks for the yearly price, and **Request it monthly instead**. Team also asks how many seats you need, from three.

On the [pricing page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/pricing) the same button takes you to the plan page, or to sign
in first if you are signed out.

## What happens after you press it

- One press files a request. The card then shows **Requested on** and the date, so you can see it was received.
- The ClearHire team reviews the request in the admin Plans queue and sets the plan up on your account.
- When it is granted, the card shows **Granted on** and the date, and the plan's limits apply straight away.
- If the team does not set it up this time, the card says so, and you can request again later.

You can have one open request at a time. Pressing the button again while one is open sends nothing new and tells you
a request is already open.

## What a paid plan changes

A paid plan raises limits, such as how many saved resumes or job alerts you can keep, and Team adds shared seats with
one person managing them. It never changes credibility: a role countersigned on Free is identical to one countersigned
on a paid plan. The current limits and prices are on the [pricing page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/pricing).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to enter card details?
No. A request needs only the plan, the period and, for Team, the number of seats.

### Can I request a plan from the Android app?
The Android app shows which plan you are on. Plans are requested on the web, at `/settings/plan`.

### What happens to my data if a plan ends?
Nothing you have already made is deleted when a plan ends. Your account goes back to the Free limits.
