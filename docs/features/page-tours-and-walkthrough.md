# ClearHire Page Tours and the How ClearHire Works Page

ClearHire gives you two ways to learn the product without leaving it. A **page tour** explains the page you are on, one
part at a time, and starts only when you ask. **[How ClearHire works](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/how-clearhire-works)**
is a public page that covers the whole product in six steps, each with a screenshot. Both are on the web app and on a
phone.

## Where the tour button is

Each page with a tour has a **Take a tour of this page** button in its header, just before the appearance control. It is
in the same place on the public pages, the document pages and inside your workspace.

The first time you open one of those pages, a small hint points at the button: *New here? Take a quick tour of this
page.* It shows once per page. ClearHire remembers that it has shown on this device and, when you are signed in, on your
account too, so a second device does not show it again. The hint leaves by itself after a few seconds. **Show me**
starts the tour, and the dismiss control closes the hint.

## Moving through a tour

- The step card says **Step n of N**. **Back** and **Next** move one step; the last step reads **Done**.
- **All steps** lists every step, so you can jump straight to the one you want.
- **Skip tour**, the close button, or the Escape key ends the tour at any step. You can start it again from the button
  at any time.
- On a phone the step card is a sheet at the bottom of the screen, and the part of the page it explains scrolls into
  view.
- The step number is kept in the address (`?tour=2`), so you can share a link that opens a tour at a given step.

## The How ClearHire works page

[How ClearHire works](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/how-clearhire-works) is public, so you can read it before you
create an account. Its six steps:

1. **Claim your roles.** Add each role you held, then ask the company to countersign it. A role reads as verified only
   once the company has said yes. See [employment verification](/features/employment-verification).
2. **Build a resume.** Start from your profile, so nothing is typed twice, then share a link or download a PDF.
3. **Find a job.** Narrow by pay, place and kind of work, and save a search you run often.
4. **Track your applications.** Every job you applied for, here or anywhere else.
5. **Use the tools.** An ATS check, cover letters, the STAR method and more. Several work before you sign up.
6. **Pick a plan.** Verified employment is on the free plan, and it stays there.

Each step has a screenshot taken at desktop and phone size, and one or two buttons that take you to that part of the
product. The current step is kept in the address (`?step=3`); the arrow keys and a swipe move between steps.

## FAQ

### Do the tours change anything in my account?
No. A tour only points at parts of the page. The one thing ClearHire stores is which pages have already shown you the
hint, so it is not shown twice.

### I dismissed the hint. How do I start a tour now?
Press **Take a tour of this page** in the header. The button stays there whether or not the hint has been shown.

### Can I read How ClearHire works without signing in?
Yes. It is a public page at `/how-clearhire-works`. Its last step offers **Create an account** if you want one.

### Do tours work on a phone?
Yes. The button is in the phone header too, and the steps open as a bottom sheet that fits a small screen.
