# Moving from resume.io to ClearHire

resume.io is quick to a polished template, and it is sold on a short trial: $2.95 for seven days, then $29.95 every four weeks unless you stop it. On the free plan you get one resume, one cover letter, and downloads in TXT only.

If paying for the PDF is fine and speed is what you want, resume.io is built for that. ClearHire suits someone who wants the PDF without a subscription running underneath it, and a role on it that the company has countersigned.

## Moving across

1. **Download the TXT.** It is what the free plan gives, and plain text pastes cleanly. If you took the trial, the PDF is yours too; note the date, because the page says the trial renews after seven days.
2. **Copy your cover letter out as well.** resume.io’s free plan holds one, and ClearHire letters are pasted in, not imported.
3. **Sign in with Google.** Then [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile), pasting each role from the TXT. The [quick start](/getting-started/quick-start) covers the first screen.
4. **Make the resume.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume). The free plan holds 30, so a version per job type is fine.
5. **Send a countersign request for each role.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). If a company says no, that answer stays between you and them.
6. **Export the PDF.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx). No trial is involved.
