# Moving from Zety to ClearHire

Zety suggests wording for each section as you write, and its paid tier downloads PDF, Word and TXT. It is sold through a 14-day trial at $1.95 that renews at $25.95 every four weeks. Its pricing page does not say what the free tier downloads.

For someone who wants help with the words and needs a Word file, Zety does that. ClearHire is for someone who would rather not have a subscription renewing every four weeks, and wants a role on the page that a company countersigned.

## Moving across

1. **Download the Word file while you are on the paid tier.** Zety lists PDF, Word and TXT there. Word copies best.
2. **Keep the suggested wording you accepted.** It is in your file now, and it is yours to paste.
3. **Sign in with Google and paste your roles into your profile.** No importer, so this is by hand, once. [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile) and the [quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).
4. **Lay out the resume.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume).
5. **Request a countersignature for each role.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). A request the company ignores lapses after its window; you can send it again.
6. **Export, open it, and only then deal with the Zety renewal.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
