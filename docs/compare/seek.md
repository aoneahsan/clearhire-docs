# Moving from SEEK to ClearHire

SEEK is where most applicants in Australia and New Zealand already are. It sells Basic, Advanced and Premium job ads; the page lists what each tier includes but not what it costs. If you hire there, staying on SEEK is the sensible default.

ClearHire publishes its price, lets you post, and gives candidates a filter for companies that confirm employment.

## If you hire on SEEK

1. **Keep your SEEK ads running.** The audience is the point.
2. **Add the company here.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies), with each office as a location.
3. **Post the same role.** By hand; there is no import.
4. **Answer countersign requests from past staff.** If your people use SEEK Pass, see [that guide](/compare/seek-pass): its check is a different thing.

## If you job hunt on SEEK

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Track SEEK applications here.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Search ClearHire with the verified filter.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
