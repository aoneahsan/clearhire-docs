# Moving from VisualCV to ClearHire

VisualCV builds a resume and hosts it at its own link, from a large template set. The builder is free; on the pricing page, the PDF download sits on Pro. If the hosted page is the thing you send people, VisualCV does that job and ClearHire does not try to.

ClearHire starts from the other end. You write your roles down once, a company you worked for countersigns them, and the resume is printed from that record. The PDF and Word file are both on the free plan, with JSON, HTML, Markdown and XML beside them.

## Moving across

1. **Take the Word file, not the PDF.** VisualCV’s builder page lists PDF, Word and Text downloads, but its pricing page marks PDF as Pro only. Word is the easier one to copy from anyway.
2. **Leave your VisualCV link live.** Anyone you already sent it to will still open it. Turn it off later, once they have the new file.
3. **Sign in with Google and fill in your profile.** There is no importer, so you paste each role in from the Word file. [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start) and [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile) walk through it.
4. **Lay out a resume from the profile.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume). If you used several VisualCV versions for different jobs, make each one here as its own resume; all of them read the same profile.
5. **Ask your past employers to countersign.** Start with the role a recruiter will look at first. [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
6. **Download and check it.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).

## What you give up

- **The hosted page.** VisualCV’s live resume link has no match here. A ClearHire resume goes out as a file.
- **A line at the foot of free exports.** A free ClearHire file prints one “built with” line; [Paid plans](/features/paid-plans) take it off.
- **Cover letters only leave as plain text.** Six formats are for resumes. Letters copy out as text.
