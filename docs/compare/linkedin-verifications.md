# Moving from LinkedIn workplace verification to ClearHire

LinkedIn’s workplace verification puts a badge on your profile. You get it through a current work email, Microsoft Entra Verified ID, or a licence your employer provides, and it costs nothing. LinkedIn says it is not available at every company. If your current employer is covered and your reader is on LinkedIn anyway, the badge does the job.

ClearHire is for the roles a work email cannot reach: the ones you have already left. A named person at the company answers, and the countersignature prints on the resume file, not only a profile.

## Moving across

The badge stays on LinkedIn. Nothing about it carries over, so each role is asked for again here.

1. **Sign in with Google.** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).
2. **Add your past roles first.** Those are the ones LinkedIn’s page does not say it covers. [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
3. **Send each company a request.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification) explains what the person on their side sees.
4. **Add your current role too,** if you want one record that holds all of them.
5. **Turn on the verified strip when you export.** Each countersigned role then prints “Countersigned by” with the company and date. [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
6. **Keep the LinkedIn badge.** It costs nothing to leave it.

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **A company has to answer.** A badge from a work email needs nobody’s time. Ours needs a person, and a request nobody answers lapses.
- **What a company writes about a person stays private** to that person and the company’s admins.
