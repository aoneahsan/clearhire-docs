# Certn and ClearHire

Certn runs background screening. Its employment verification, $29.99, contacts past employers to confirm titles and dates; current employers only if permitted. If you are an employer who needs criminal record, identity and employment checks in one order, Certn does that.

ClearHire keeps the past employer’s answer on the person’s own record, free, with the name of who answered.

## There is nothing to move

A Certn result belongs to the employer who ordered it. It does not come across.

- **Your roles:** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). Each answer prints “Countersigned by” on an [export](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx) when you ask it to.
- **Your company:** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **Roles only.** No criminal record or identity check.
- **The company can change its answer later.** Once a role is countersigned, you cannot edit it, but the company that answered can change its own answer.
