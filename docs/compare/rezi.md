# Moving from Rezi to ClearHire

Rezi writes for you: AI bullet points, an AI interview, and unlimited cover and resignation letters. The free tier allows one resume and three PDF downloads. Pro is $29 a month, or there is a lifetime option at $149.

If having the bullets written is the main thing, and a one-off price appeals, Rezi fits. ClearHire gives unlimited exports on the free plan in six formats, and a role on the page that somebody else signed.

## Moving across

1. **Use one of your three free PDF downloads.** If you are on Pro or Lifetime, the Word file is the better pick.
2. **Keep the bullets.** Whatever Rezi wrote that you kept is your text now. Paste it.
3. **Sign in with Google and fill in your profile.** No importer. [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile), starting from the [quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).
4. **Build the resume.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume).
5. **Get the roles countersigned.** This is the part no writing tool can do for you: a named company says yes. [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
6. **Export as often as you like.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
