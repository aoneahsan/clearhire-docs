# Moving from Glassdoor for Employers to ClearHire

Glassdoor for Employers is employer branding: featured reviews, your responses, and hiring bundled through Indeed. If reviews already shape how candidates see you, answering them where they are read is worth doing, and no price is shown on the page.

ClearHire publishes its price. Its company page carries a mark that means you answer employment claims, and reviews here are tied to roles a company countersigned.

## If you manage your brand on Glassdoor

1. **Keep responding there.** The audience reading those reviews is not going to move.
2. **Create the company on ClearHire.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
3. **Post roles here.** Glassdoor sends hiring to Indeed.
4. **Answer employment claims.** The mark on your page comes from that, not from a paid tier.
5. **Read the reviews written about you here.** They are public once a moderator clears them.

## If you look for work

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Track applications from both.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Use the verified filter.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
