# Comparably and ClearHire

Comparably collects anonymous culture ratings and reviews, split by role and demographic. Employers can claim their page with a company email; its premium employer tier is sold through sales. For culture scores left anonymously, it is the specialist.

On ClearHire, a review sits beside a role the company countersigned, and both sides can write.

## There is nothing to move

Ratings on Comparably are anonymous and stay there.

- **Review a company here** once your role is countersigned: [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification), then [Company reviews](/features/company-reviews).
- **The countersigned role itself** goes out on your [resume export](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **No culture scores by demographic.**
- **The company’s side is private.** What a company writes about a person reaches only that person and its admins.
