# Moving from LinkedIn Jobs to ClearHire

LinkedIn Jobs puts a post beside the network your candidates already use. Its price depends on your hiring needs; the page gives no rate card. If the people you want are on LinkedIn every day, that matters more than anything below.

ClearHire publishes its price, gives candidates a filter for companies that confirm employment, and shows roles a company has countersigned.

## If you hire on LinkedIn

1. **Create the company in ClearHire.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
2. **Post the role by hand.** No LinkedIn import exists.
3. **Watch the price you pay on each side.** Ours is on [Paid plans](/features/paid-plans); LinkedIn’s comes from their sales flow.
4. **Answer employment claims as they arrive.** Each countersign you give is one more former employee whose record shows your company’s answer.

## If you look for work on LinkedIn

1. **Retype your history.** Your LinkedIn profile does not import. [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Track LinkedIn applications here too.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Search ClearHire’s board with the verified filter on.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
4. **Keep your LinkedIn profile.** Recruiters will still look there first.
