# Glassdoor reviews and ClearHire

Glassdoor has company and interview reviews at scale, and employers answer them from a Glassdoor employer account. If you want to read what an interview was like before you go, Glassdoor has more of those than anyone.

A ClearHire review is tied to a role the company countersigned, and the company can write about the person as well.

## There is nothing to move

Your Glassdoor reviews stay on Glassdoor.

- **Write one here** after the role is countersigned: [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification), then [Company reviews](/features/company-reviews). The role also prints on a [PDF or Word resume](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).

## What Glassdoor’s pages say

Read on 7 November 2026. Only the employer page was readable; the community guidelines returned a security check. Nothing we read mentioned companies writing about people, or role confirmation. Every row’s source is on <a href="https://www.glassdoor.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Glassdoor reviews’s site</a>.

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **No interview reviews.**
- **A company’s answer about a person stays private** to them and the company’s admins.
