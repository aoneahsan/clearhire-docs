# Moving from LiveCareer to ClearHire

LiveCareer offers ready-made content and, on its paid plan, PDF, Word, TXT and RTF downloads. The free version downloads TXT only. Paid starts with a $2.95 14-day trial that renews at $23.95 every four weeks, or $71.40 a year paid up front.

If you already pay for LiveCareer and need an RTF file, staying is reasonable. ClearHire is for someone who wants the PDF and Word file on a free plan, and a role a company has countersigned.

## Moving across

1. **Download what your plan allows.** TXT on free; Word if you are paying.
2. **Strip the ready-made lines you never meant to keep.** Pre-written content creeps in. Moving is a good moment to read every bullet once.
3. **Sign in with Google and paste into your profile.** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start), [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile). No importer.
4. **Build the resume.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume).
5. **Ask each company to countersign.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
6. **Export as Word or PDF.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx). There is no RTF.
