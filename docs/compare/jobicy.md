# Moving from Jobicy to ClearHire

Jobicy sells a remote listing once, for 30 days: $190 basic or $290 boosted, no subscription. Only employer accounts can publish, and the account is checked first. A known price and nothing recurring is the appeal.

ClearHire’s post is free, and candidates can filter to companies that answer employment claims.

## If you post on Jobicy

1. **Let the current 30 days run.**
2. **Add the company on ClearHire.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
3. **Post the role again here.** No import.
4. **Answer countersign requests from past staff.**

## If you look for remote work on Jobicy

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Track Jobicy applications here.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Search with the verified filter on.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
