# Moving from Teal to ClearHire

Teal is a resume builder and a job tracker together, with keyword matching against each job description. Most of it is free forever, with unlimited resumes; Teal+ is billed weekly at $13, or $29 for thirty days.

People who live in Teal usually live in the tracker. If matching keywords and tracking in one place is your routine, Teal is built around it. ClearHire names its export formats, has its own job board with a filter for companies that confirm employment, and puts countersigned roles on the resume.

## Moving across

1. **Start with the tracker, not the resume.** Write down every application that is still open: company, role, stage.
2. **Add those to ClearHire’s tracker.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking) holds jobs found anywhere. Closed ones can stay in Teal.
3. **Pick the resume you trust most.** Teal makes many versions easy. Copy the text out of that one; the pricing page does not name download formats.
4. **Sign in with Google and paste it into your profile.** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start) and [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile). No importer.
5. **Rebuild the versions you still use.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume). Each draws on the one profile.
6. **Ask for countersignatures.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
7. **Export.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
