# Moving from Remote OK to ClearHire

Remote OK gets a remote post live from one form, with the salary range shown. If the company email matches the company domain, the post gets a Verified tag. A brand-colour highlight costs $499 extra.

ClearHire’s employer mark is a different promise: a named person at the company answers employment claims. That is slower to earn than a domain match, and it means more.

## If you post on Remote OK

1. **Set up your company.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
2. **Post the role, salary range included.** Same habit as Remote OK’s form.
3. **Name someone to answer claims.** That person, not your email domain, is what earns the mark.

## If you look for remote work there

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Move your open applications into the tracker.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Turn on the verified filter.** [Job search](/features/job-search). Fewer listings, each from a company that answers.
