# Xref and ClearHire

Xref automates reference checking for employers: surveys, reminders and reports, priced monthly by company size from $70 a month. It has no free plan. If you run many reference checks a month, it saves a lot of chasing.

A ClearHire record belongs to the person. It is confirmed once, and every later employer reads the same answer without a new check.

## There is nothing to move

Xref’s references are gathered for the employer who asked. They stay with that employer.

- **Start your own record:** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). The next employer reads the answer on your [resume file](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
- **Answer for your company:** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **A countersignature is not a reference.** It confirms the role. It does not say how well you did it.
- **Any company answer about a person** is private to that person and the company’s admins.
