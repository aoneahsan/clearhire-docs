# Checkr and ClearHire

Checkr runs background checks for employers: criminal, identity, and employment verification as an add-on priced by how far back it looks, from $12.50 for a current employer to $50 for ten years. If you are the employer and need a check on a candidate after the offer, Checkr does that.

ClearHire is for proof that exists before the offer, costs the person nothing, and is theirs to show.

## There is nothing to move

The employer orders a Checkr report; the candidate does not own it. Nothing from it comes across.

- **Your own history:** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). Bring it to the interview as a [PDF or Word file](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
- **Your company, being named on claims:** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **No criminal or identity checks.** Only roles.
- **The company may say no.** A declined claim stays private, but the role stays unconfirmed.
