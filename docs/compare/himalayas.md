# Moving from Himalayas to ClearHire

Himalayas is a remote job board. Posting is free once the company profile is filled in, and company access goes through a work email. Job seekers can pay for Plus at $9 a month or Max at $29.

If you hire remotely and want a free post with a company profile beside it, Himalayas gives you both. ClearHire lets candidates filter to companies that confirm employment, and apply with countersigned roles.

## If you hire on Himalayas

1. **Copy your company profile across.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies) has the same idea: profile, locations, branding.
2. **Repost the role.** No import.
3. **Answer the countersign requests from remote alumni.** Distributed teams often have no one else who can.

## If you job hunt on Himalayas

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Bring your applications into the tracker.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Filter to remote roles at companies that confirm employment.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
4. **Keep Plus or Max only if you use what they add.** None of it is needed here.
