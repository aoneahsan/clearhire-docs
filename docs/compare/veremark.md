# Veremark and ClearHire

Veremark sells pre-employment checks to hiring teams: employment verification from $20, or a $144 Professional package with three years of employment and two references. If you hire across countries and want employment, reference and sanctions checks from one account, Veremark covers that.

ClearHire keeps the confirmation with the person, free, and shows it to whoever they share it with.

## There is nothing to move

Each Veremark check belongs to the hiring team that bought it. None comes across.

- **For your own roles:** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification), then share the [exported resume](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx), which names who countersigned.
- **If people name your company:** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
