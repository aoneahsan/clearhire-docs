# Veremark and ClearHire

**Veremark:** Pre-employment checks for hiring teams, with employment verification sold per candidate. This page is the practical half: what carries across, in what order, and where it stops. For this one, very little does.

## Who should stay, and who should move

Choose Veremark if you hire across countries and want employment, reference and sanctions checks from one account.

Choose ClearHire if you want the confirmation free, held by the person, and visible to any reader they share it with.

## There is nothing to move

Veremark sells a check to the employer or lender who orders it. ClearHire keeps a record that belongs to the person it is about. One does not replace the other, and no Veremark report comes across.

- If the history is yours, start with [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
- If you run a company and people name it on their claims, start with [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **Only resumes leave in six formats.** A [resume exports](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx) as PDF, Word, JSON, HTML, Markdown or XML. Cover letters copy out as plain text, and a portfolio has no export.
- **Free exports name us.** A resume exported on Free carries one “built with” line. [Paid plans](/features/paid-plans) remove it.
- **There is no career path on your record.** The roadmap and skills-gap pages are reading, not a plan kept for you.
- **A company’s answer about a person stays private.** Only that person and the company’s admins can read it.
