# Blind and ClearHire

Blind is an anonymous workplace community. It describes its members as verified professionals and carries anonymous company reviews and ratings. If you want frank talk with people inside the same companies, Blind is that.

ClearHire is the opposite end: a named company’s answer on your own record, and a review tied to a countersigned role.

## There is nothing to move

Blind is anonymous by design. There is nothing of yours to bring.

- **Start a named record:** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
- **Review a company under your countersigned role:** [Company reviews](/features/company-reviews).
- **Show the record outside ClearHire:** the [resume export](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx) carries it.

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **Nothing here is anonymous.**
- **A company’s answer about a person is private** to them and the company’s admins.
