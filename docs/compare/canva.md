# Moving from Canva to ClearHire

Canva is a design tool that happens to have resume templates. You get full visual control, and the free plan exports PDF, JPG and PNG. If your resume is one design among many you make, Canva is the right place for it.

ClearHire is not a canvas. You enter your roles as a record, a company countersigns them, and resumes are printed from that record in Word, PDF and data formats. You trade visual freedom for structure.

## Moving across

1. **Copy the text box by box inside Canva.** A designed resume often splits one role across several boxes, and a PDF of it can paste out of order.
2. **Or download the PDF and check the order** before you paste anything.
3. **Sign in with Google and enter each role in your profile.** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start), [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile). No importer.
4. **Pick a template.** [Build your first resume](/getting-started/build-your-first-resume). Moving text boxes around the page is not something it does.
5. **Ask for countersignatures.** [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
6. **Export.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
7. **Keep Canva.** You will still want it for everything that is not a resume.
