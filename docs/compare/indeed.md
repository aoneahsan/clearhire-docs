# Moving from Indeed to ClearHire

Indeed is about reach. An employer starts with a free post and can sponsor it on a daily or monthly budget, paying when candidates engage. Nobody needs to leave Indeed for ClearHire, and this page does not pretend otherwise.

What ClearHire adds is narrower: a job-board filter that shows only companies that answer employment claims, and applicants whose past roles a company has countersigned.

## If you hire on Indeed

1. **Set up your company here.** Sign in with Google and fill in the profile and locations. [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
2. **Retype the posting.** Indeed postings do not copy over, and we have no importer for them. Add the salary range: it feeds the salary filter candidates use.
3. **Answer the claims that name you.** People who worked for you can ask you to countersign. Doing so is what puts your company inside the “companies that confirm employment” filter.
4. **Keep sponsoring on Indeed if reach matters.** Run both.

## If you look for work on Indeed

1. **Make a profile once.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Copy your open Indeed applications into the tracker.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking) takes jobs from anywhere, so Indeed and ClearHire jobs sit in one list.
3. **Try the verified filter on the board.** [Job search](/features/job-search) explains its three settings.
4. **Carry on applying on Indeed.** The tracker is where the two meet.
