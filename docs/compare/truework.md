# Truework and ClearHire

Truework verifies employment and income for lenders, at $59.95 for employment or $64.95 with income, billed per verification. If you are a lender checking a borrower, it is built for you.

ClearHire is for the person whose history it is. They have it confirmed once, free, and it shows on their own record.

## There is nothing to move

A Truework verification is a report a lender ordered. It does not belong to the borrower, and none comes across.

- **If it is your own history,** start at [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification). A countersigned role then prints on any [resume export](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx) with the verified strip on.
- **If your company gets asked to confirm former staff,** see [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).

## Where ClearHire falls short

- **No income.** A countersignature covers the role and dates. Nothing about pay.
- **A company’s answer about a person** is private to them and the company’s admins.
