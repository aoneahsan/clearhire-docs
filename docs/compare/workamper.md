# Moving from Workamper News to ClearHire

Workamper News is a membership site for RV work-campers in the US, with a resume builder that employers search. Memberships start at $5. If you live on the road and want the seasonal employers who already look there, keep it.

ClearHire is a free record of any role in any trade, countersigned by the company. A season at a campground counts the same as an office job.

## Moving across

Nothing on Workamper News carries over, and we could not read whether it confirms entries at all.

1. **Sign in with Google.** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).
2. **Enter each season as its own role,** with the start and end dates. [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
3. **Ask the park or company to countersign each one.** Roles you have already left are fine. [Request employment verification](/guides/request-employment-verification).
4. **Export the resume to carry with you.** [Export as PDF or DOCX](/guides/export-resume-pdf-docx).
5. **Keep your Workamper membership** for the employers who search it.
