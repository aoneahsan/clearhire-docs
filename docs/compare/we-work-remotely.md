# Moving from We Work Remotely to ClearHire

We Work Remotely is a long-running remote board selling single posts at $299, bulk posts from $179 each, and Mega Placements at $1,999. Hiring for one role at a time, on a board remote workers have known for years, is what it is good at.

On ClearHire, candidates can filter to companies that answer employment claims, and the post is free.

## If you post on We Work Remotely

1. **Use up any bulk posts you have bought.** The page says they are good for a year.
2. **Add the company here.** [ClearHire for companies](/features/for-companies).
3. **Post the next role on both** and compare who applies.
4. **Answer countersign requests.** That is what puts you in the verified filter.

## If you look for work there

1. **Profile.** [Create your profile](/getting-started/create-your-profile).
2. **Track your We Work Remotely applications here.** [Application tracking](/features/applications-tracking).
3. **Search with the verified filter.** [Job search](/features/job-search).
