# ClearHire white-label licence

**A ClearHire white-label licence is ClearHire running under your organisation's name, on your domain, hosted and
updated by us.** You offer it to your own students, candidates or members. The source code stays with us unless you
buy it outright, which is a separate purchase called the buyout.

It suits a university careers office, a recruitment agency, or an HR or staffing firm that wants career tools, a
resume builder and countersigned employment records with its own name on the door.

## What is delivered for each platform

A licence covers the platforms you pay for. ClearHire ships two today, so those are the two you can license.

| Platform | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Web app | The whole ClearHire web app on your domain over HTTPS: the career tools, the resume builder and countersigned employment records, with your name, logo and colours on every page and every email it sends. |
| Android + iOS app | The Android app under your name, published in your own Play account. iOS follows when you provide or fund an Apple developer account. |

There is no browser extension, npm package or desktop app to license, because ClearHire does not ship one.

## Licence or buyout

- **A licence** is non-exclusive. We host and update it, and we may license ClearHire to other buyers until someone
  buys it outright. No source code is disclosed.
- **The buyout** hands over the source code of every repository that builds the web and Android apps, with six
  months of updates. After it, ClearHire is not sold to anyone else, and the buyer pays us a 7% royalty on its gross
  revenue, quarterly, for as long as it earns.

Prices, the bundle discount and the buyout figure are on the [pricing page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing),
which reads them from our licensing service. They are not repeated here, so this page cannot disagree with it.

## Changes only you need

A feature only your users need, or an integration with a system you already run, is custom work. It is quoted and
paid separately and never folded into a licence. Ask on the [custom work page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
