# Setup, updates and renewals

**Setup is the part between paying and your users signing in; updates are what keeps your copy current after
that.** This page covers both, in the order they happen.

## From payment to a live copy

1. **We write to you** to agree the domain, the logo and the colours before anything is deployed.
2. **We deploy your copy** on your domain with your name on it, and put its licence file up beside it at
   `/.well-known/clearhire-white-label-license.json`.
3. **The licence key turns active.** Until then it reads *pending setup* on the
   [verify page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/verify-license), which is the correct state and not a fault.

A licence is issued when the payment provider confirms the payment to our licensing service. The page you return to
after paying grants nothing by itself; the licence key arrives by email.

## What counts as an update

| Included | Separate |
| --- | --- |
| Fixes, security patches and the features ClearHire itself gains, deployed to your copy | Changes made only for you, which are [custom work](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work) |
| New releases of the Android app, if you hold the mobile platform | Your own domain, store accounts and any paid service you add |

## The included six months

Every licence and every buyout comes with six months of product updates, counted from the purchase date. When they
end, your deployment keeps running on the version it has. Nothing is switched off.

## Renewing

Renew on the [updates page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email
the licence was issued to, for three, six or twelve more months. A renewal starts from whichever is later: today,
or the day your current updates end, so nothing you already paid for is cut short. The renewal prices are on that
page, read from our licensing service.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can we change the domain after paying?
Yes, before we deploy: reply to the setup email with the new one. The licence is issued for the domain your copy actually runs on.

### What happens if we do not renew?
Your copy keeps running on its last version. Renewing later brings the updates back from the renewal date.

### Does a buyer of the source get updates too?
Yes. A buyout includes six months of updates, and renewals stay open to the buyer afterwards.
