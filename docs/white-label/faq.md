# White-label questions

**These are the questions organisations ask before buying a ClearHire white-label licence or the source.** The
binding text is the White-label section of the [Terms of Service](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/terms#c14).

## The code

### Do we get the source code with a licence?
No. A licence is the product running under your name on our hosting. The code changes hands only with a buyout.

### What does a buyout include?
The source code of every repository that builds the web and Android apps, with six months of updates. After it,
ClearHire is not sold to anyone else. Licences sold before the buyout keep their terms.

## Exclusivity

### Can someone else license ClearHire too?
Yes, until somebody buys it outright. A licence is non-exclusive, and the buyout is the only exclusive thing sold.

## Platforms

### Which platforms can we license?
The web app and the mobile app. The mobile app means Android and iOS together; iOS is delivered when you provide or
fund an Apple developer account. ClearHire has no browser extension, package or desktop app to license.

### Can we buy the mobile app without the web app?
Yes. Each platform is priced on its own, and buying both takes a bundle discount shown on the
[pricing page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing).

## Changes and costs

### Can you change it for us?
Yes, as custom work, quoted and paid separately from the licence. Ask on the
[custom work page](https://clearhire.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

### Who pays for the domain and store accounts?
You do. Costs you choose to take on, such as your domain, your store accounts and paid services you add, are yours.
